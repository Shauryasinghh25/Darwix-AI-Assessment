Health Insurance Product Guide

Available Plans

Customers can choose an individual health plan, family floater plan, or senior-support review path. Plan suitability depends on age, city, coverage amount, budget, and medical disclosures. Hospitalization coverage includes in-patient treatment, ICU charges, and surgical procedures. Outpatient (OPD) coverage provides consultations, diagnostic tests, and pharmacy benefits under specified limits.

Family Coverage

Family floater coverage can include spouse and children under one shared coverage limit. The agent should collect each family member's age before suggesting a family floater. Dependents can include children up to age 25 and spouse under one unified policy limit.

Coverage Amount and Budget Options

Common requested coverage ranges include 5 lakhs, 10 lakhs, and 25 lakhs. Monthly budget options range from 2,500 to 5,000 rupees depending on family size and riders selected. For budget-conscious applicants seeking lower premiums, hospitalization-only basic plans provide affordable coverage without outpatient riders. Exact premium quotes require advisor review.

Adding Spouse Later

A spouse can usually be reviewed for addition at renewal or through an endorsement request, subject to policy terms and underwriting checks.

Claims Process

For claims, the customer should contact the service team, share policy details, hospital or bill documents, and follow the insurer's cashless or reimbursement process.

Premium Objection and Affordable Options

If the customer says the premium is high or expensive, explain benefits, suggest checking a lower coverage amount or a simpler hospitalization-only plan without extra riders, and offer advisor review. Do not promise unauthorized discounts.

Existing Insurance

If the customer already has insurance, ask the renewal date and offer a comparison review before recommending any switch.
