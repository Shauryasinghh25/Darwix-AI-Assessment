Insurance Policy Renewal Guide

Policy renewal notice is sent 30 days before expiry. What happens during policy renewal: policyholders receive a notice and have a grace period of 30 days to pay the renewal premium. If premium remains unpaid after the grace period, the policy may lapse.
Note: For queries about "renwel" or renewal grace period, policyholders retain continuous coverage during the 30-day grace period.

Coverage continues only when the premium is received within the allowed period.
